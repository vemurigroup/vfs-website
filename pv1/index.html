      Child Education

      A goal with a fixed deadline.

      Unlike retirement, a child's education date doesn't move — which makes it one of the clearest goals to plan for: a target amount, a target year, and a SIP sized to get there.

      How we approach it

      1

      Cost today, cost at admission

      Education inflation runs ahead of general inflation — we work out what today's cost becomes by the year your child needs it. The inflation calculator shows the same math.

      2

      A dedicated SIP, not a side pot

      A separate SIP mapped to this one goal, so it doesn't get raided for something else along the way.

      3

      De-risk as the date nears

      Equity while the horizon is long, shifting to debt/hybrid in the last 2–3 years so a bad year in the market doesn't collide with the admission deadline.

      Help

      Common questions about generating and paying for signed invoices.

      What is an ARN?

      Your AMFI Registration Number. It's how we manage your subscription and label every invoice we generate for you.

      Where do I get the files to upload?

      From your registrar portal — KFIN or CAMS. You need two things each month: the unsigned invoice ZIP and the Excel template.

      What signature should I upload?

      A PNG on a transparent or plain white background. Every ARN keeps its own signature, set once on the Signatory tab.

      Why does the preview have a watermark?

      Previews are free and watermarked so you can check the output before committing. Downloads are free for your first month, then draw on invoice credits — or go unlimited on Professional.

      Is my first month really free?

      Yes — a genuine 30-day trial: unlimited KFIN and CAMS invoices, GST returns, and Money In, no card required. It starts the moment you verify your email.

      What happens after the free month?

      Nothing is charged automatically, and nothing is deleted. Keep going pay-as-you-go with a Starter Pack (100 credits for ₹499 + GST), or switch to Professional for unlimited use. Everything you've already generated stays in your account — sign in any time to redownload past invoices.

      What is an invoice credit?

      After your free month, on pay-as-you-go, one credit signs one fund-house invoice. A Starter Pack adds 100 credits for ₹499 + GST, and they never expire — a credit is only spent when a run actually succeeds.

      How do I go unlimited?

      Switch to Professional from Billing — ₹599/month or ₹1,999/year + GST, launch pricing that won't stay this low. We raise a GST invoice, and once payment is confirmed, invoices are unlimited for as long as the plan stays active.

      Can I change my ARN?

      ARN changes go through support review. Request one from the Generate tab and it takes effect from your next billing cycle.

      12 — KYC & Regulatory

      The paperwork, and who we are on paper.

      KYC is a SEBI-mandated, one-time process every investor completes before investing in mutual funds. And since we distribute rather than manage your money, here's exactly who does, and how to escalate past us.

      KYC steps

      The process, in short

      1

      New KYC

      First-time investors register PAN, address and identity proof once, through any SEBI-registered intermediary — it then works across every mutual fund you invest in.

      2

      Re-KYC

      A periodic re-verification SEBI requires to keep records current — typically triggered by a change in address, contact details, or KYC status flags.

      3

      CKYC

      The Central KYC Registry — a shared KYC record used across SEBI-regulated intermediaries (mutual funds, insurers, banks), so you don't repeat the same paperwork everywhere.

      Related checks

      4

      FATCA self-declaration

      A US tax-compliance declaration required from every investor regardless of nationality, done once during onboarding.

      5

      Nomination

      Mandatory (or an explicit opt-out) on every folio — the fastest way to make sure your investments reach the right person without a legal dispute.

      6

      Bank mandate

      Your registered bank account for both investing and redemptions — keep it current so payouts and SIP debits don't bounce.

      Documents you'll typically need

      PAN card, an address proof (Aadhaar, passport, voter ID or utility bill), and a recent photograph. Some intermediaries also ask for an in-person or video verification step.

      KYC FAQs

      Do I need to redo KYC for every fund house?

      No. KYC done through a KRA (KYC Registration Agency) is valid across every SEBI-registered intermediary — that's the point of CKYC.

      When would I need Re-KYC?

      If your KYC status shows as "on hold" or "registered but not verified," or if key details like address or ID proof have changed since you first registered.

      Is FATCA declaration only for NRIs?

      No — every investor, resident or non-resident, must complete a FATCA/CRS self-declaration as part of onboarding.

      Can I check my KYC status myself?

      Yes, using the "Validate my KYC status" link above with your PAN — no need to call anyone.
